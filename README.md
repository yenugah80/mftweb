MFT is a world-class, multimodal nutritional assistant designed to make food tracking as natural as a conversation, it transforms how users interact with their health data through voice, vision, and text.

Core Experience
Live Voice Interaction: Using the Gemini Live API, the app provides a low-latency, "always-on" conversational experience. You can speak to the app to describe a meal, and it responds with human-like audio while simultaneously rendering deep data visualizations.

Multimodal Intelligence: Beyond just text, it uses Gemini's vision capabilities to analyze food photos. It identifies not just the dish, but the cooking method (e.g., steaming vs. deep-frying) and intelligently adjusts micronutrient estimates based on heat-sensitive vitamin loss (like Vitamin C and B-vitamins).
Deep Ingredient Breakdown: It doesn't just give total calories; it dissects the meal into individual components, providing a "bill of materials" for your food, each with its own specific macro profile.

Visual & Technical Sophistication
Dynamic Visualizations: Powered by Recharts, the app features interactive pie charts for macros and specialized bar charts for micronutrients that prioritize % Daily Value, helping users identify nutritional gaps instantly.

Metabolic Insights: The UI explicitly highlights "Net Carbs," providing a better understanding of glycemic impact, alongside hydration and electrolyte tracking.

Comparative Analysis: A robust history system allows users to select any two meals from their past and generate a side-by-side comparison to determine which was the healthier choice.
Premium UI/UX: Built with a "dark-first" aesthetic using 

Tailwind CSS, the app includes a real-time audio visualizer that reacts to your voice, smooth "animate-in" transitions, and full support for both Metric and Imperial systems.
Finally, This isn't just a calorie counter; it's a sophisticated forensic tool for your diet, providing clinical-grade insights through a beautiful, accessible interface.
